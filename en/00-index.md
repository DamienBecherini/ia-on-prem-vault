Welcome to the "Zero to Hero" Local AI guide. This Vault is designed as a digital garden: don't read it linearly. Follow your needs, click on concepts you haven't mastered yet, and build your expertise step by step.

> [!tip] Goal
> Teach you how to size, deploy, and understand the hardware and software infrastructure required to run massive AI models (LLMs) in a 100% local, private, and high-performance environment.

The content covers:

- **Foundations** — how LLM inference works, memory bandwidth, KV cache, quantization
- **Hardware** — unified memory APUs (Apple Silicon, AMD Gorgon Halo), multi-GPU stations, AI networking
- **Software stack** — inference engines (Ollama, vLLM, TensorRT-LLM), clustering, RAG & agents
- **Architecture blueprints** — four scenarios from solo dev lab to datacenter
- **Agents & Assistants** — personal AI assistants and custodian agents with data sovereignty focus
- **Implementation guides** — evaluating local models, protocols, KPIs
