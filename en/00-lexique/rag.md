## 📝 Short definition
Method that retrieves relevant documents before generating a response with an LLM.

## 📖 Detailed definition
RAG combines a search engine (often vector-based) and a generative model.
The injected context is targeted, which improves domain accuracy and reduces hallucinations.

## 💡 Why it matters for on-prem AI
It lets an organization use internal knowledge without retraining the model.
