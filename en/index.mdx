## A sourced guide, reviewed by its author

This guide is written with the help of AI agents, and its author, Damien BECHERINI, reviews and approves every change. AI tools change every week: here is how it stays current.

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="1. A script watches" icon="magnifier">
		A program with no AI records new releases, security advisories and new models.
	</Card>
	<Card title="2. An AI agent proposes" icon="pencil">
		The agent finds the passages that have become wrong and proposes corrections with their sources.
	</Card>
	<Card title="3. A human decides" icon="approve-check">
